## 🧱 FORGE Framework

My PhD thesis is unified under **FORGE** — *Framework for Out-of-distribution Robustness, Generalisation and Evaluation* — a systematic programme for diagnosing, benchmarking, and stabilising AI systems under real-world distribution shift.

| Component | Focus |
|---|---|
| **FORGE-BENCH** | Federated medical imaging robustness benchmarking (MedFL-Stress) |
| **FORGE-DIAG** | Multimodal instability diagnosis in VLMs under domain shift (DAMF) |
| **FORGE-EVAL** | Worst-client robustness evaluation in federated systems (FM Fairness Paradox, FL-NLP) |
| **FORGE-ADAPT** | Adaptive gradient-signal stage switching *(in progress — thesis Ch. 4)* |
